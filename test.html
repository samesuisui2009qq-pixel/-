1. 段落

This is a transitional juvenile/1st-w. Brown Shrike with much retained juvenile plumage. Note the short primary projection, and compare with Red-backed Shrike and Isabelline Shrike. The Northern Waterthrush and the Louisiana Waterthrush are best separated by the shape of the supercilium, the throat markings and the ground colour of the flanks.
